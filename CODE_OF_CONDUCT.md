# Community guidelines

This issue tracker is for constructive feedback about Secure. Disagreement and criticism are welcome; harassment is not.

Please:

- Discuss the issue rather than attacking the person reporting it.
- Keep reports relevant, accurate, and specific.
- Respect people's privacy. Do not publish personal information, private conversations, or security-sensitive data.
- Avoid spam, threats, harassment, discriminatory abuse, and repeated off-topic posts.
- Do not use this tracker to publish exploit details or coordinate harm.

Maintainers may edit labels, hide comments, lock discussions, or close issues when needed to keep the tracker usable. Serious or repeated violations may be reported to GitHub.

To report problematic behavior privately, contact **dev@securelegion.org**.
